Test version of the Photoframe Binding. Requires **openHAB 5.0 or later**.

## New: choose the page opened by a tap
- The new setting **Page on Tap** lists your Main UI pages and sitemaps. Pick one, no address or domain is needed. Sitemaps open in Basic UI.
- The page opens on top of the slideshow, and after **Return to Slideshow** seconds without touch the slideshow comes back.
- **Tap Address** (advanced) stays for app addresses such as `intent:…`. **Page on Tap** takes priority over it.

`&tap=` in an address overrides the settings of the thing. If your full-screen page contains `&tap=%2F`, remove it, otherwise a tap keeps opening the start page.

## Update from 0.0.11
Delete `org.openhab.binding.photoframe-0.0.11.jar` from the `addons` folder and copy `org.openhab.binding.photoframe-0.0.12.jar`.
